    I was gonna let this be, but since it’s on #61 currently on this site, I thought I should write a review for picky readers like me.
    If you wanna read this for-
    Actual CONSISTENT plot, character depth, and well-written stories, this is NOT for you.
    Why you should read it? If you’re okay with generic but funny plot. I like fl-ml chemistry overall, it’s more towards the fluffy side. art overall is good. Apart from that it’s good if you don’t mind the glaring inconsistencies and “for the sake of this trope” plot points. It does a good job in targeting your rage sometimes, so when fl resolves situation it brings you satisfaction. Maybe if you like that kind of story, you will keep reading.
    FL is dumb af, she makes advances and then out of nowhere is shy which contradicts her character. Is extremely incompetent? but I can overlook all that, i can go through stories that have dumb characters.
    What threw me off among other things- ML thinking she’s just a bystander so he kinda pities her, he thinks she’s not really present in the world. And yk what? This is the most used sentence for all reincarnation tropes BUT it doesn’t work here cuz she is actually very expressive and overall an engaging person. Nothing about her personality screams bystander.
    There’s a lot of stuff I can say but bottomline is, it feels like author is trying to shove down over-used plot points like above, plot points like wlw, no misogyny etc just cuz it’s popular(?) without writing it well. The glaring inconsistencies of what author tries to tell us and how mc acts and story unfolds is extremely contradictory which makes this manhwa hard to read. It’s good for mindless reading like most generic manhwas but not preferable if you want something refreshing or well-written.
